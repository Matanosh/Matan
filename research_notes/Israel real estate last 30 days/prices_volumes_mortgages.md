## 1. Latest CBS home price index releases

The most recent CBS Home Price Index release I could find came out with the September CPI on **Oct 15, 2026**, one day before the research window. It covers July–August 2026: prices fell 0.6% from June–July, new-home prices fell 0.4%, and prices were **1.8% lower year on year**. This reversed two months of slight rises. The next release (August–September index, published alongside October CPI) was scheduled for **Nov 15, 2026**, but I found no coverage of it.

## 2. Transaction volumes, investors vs first-time buyers, new vs second-hand

### Takeaway
On **Nov 12, 2026** the CBS published Q3 2026 figures: about **21,750–21,800 homes sold**, down about 5.7–6% from Q2 and about 7.3% year on year. New-home sales fell 12% from the previous quarter but stayed about 4% above Q3 2025. Tel Aviv new-home sales collapsed about 60% from the previous quarter. Finance Ministry data reported in the window show **investors bought 849 homes in September 2026, down 29% y/y**.

### Cited Findings
- Q3 2026 total sales (CBS, published Nov 12, 2026): "some 21,800 homes" sold, down about 6% from Q2 2026 — [Globes, "New home sales plunge in Tel Aviv" (Nov 12, 2026)](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-new-home-sales-plunge-in-tel-aviv-1001563213). i24 gives 21,750, −5.7% from Q2 and −7.3% from Q3 2025 — [i24NEWS, "Israel's housing sales fall as unsold new homes reach 85,000"](https://www.i24news.tv/en/news/israel/economy/artc-israel-s-housing-sales-fall-as-unsold-new-homes-reach-85-000)
- New-home sales in Q3 2026: −12% from Q2, but about +4% year on year — [i24NEWS](https://www.i24news.tv/en/news/israel/economy/artc-israel-s-housing-sales-fall-as-unsold-new-homes-reach-85-000) / [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-new-home-sales-plunge-in-tel-aviv-1001563213)
- Tel Aviv: new-home sales −60% in Q3 vs Q2, which drove the national decline; 958 total (new plus second-hand) homes sold in Tel Aviv in Q3 — [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-new-home-sales-plunge-in-tel-aviv-1001563213); [AgentPlus summary, "New-home sales in Tel Aviv drop 60% in Q3; Haifa tops transaction rankings"](https://agentplus-israel.com/en/news/new-home-sales-in-tel-aviv-drop-60-in-q3-haifa-tops-transaction-rankin-261112-c677)
- Haifa topped the city transaction rankings in Q3. Haifa new-apartment sales were up 17%, while second-hand sales were slightly down — [AgentPlus](https://agentplus-israel.com/en/news/new-home-sales-in-tel-aviv-drop-60-in-q3-haifa-tops-transaction-rankin-261112-c677); [Haipo (Haifa News)](https://haipo.co.il/en/item/659389)
- Investors, September 2026 (Finance Ministry): bought 849 homes, −29% y/y — [i24NEWS](https://www.i24news.tv/en/news/israel/economy/artc-israel-s-housing-sales-fall-as-unsold-new-homes-reach-85-000)
- August 2026 (Finance Ministry, reported in the Times of Israel October snapshot): 7,532 apartments sold, −1% vs August 2025. Investors bought 1,077 (−2% y/y) and sold 1,171 (−5% y/y) — [Times of Israel Oct 2026 snapshot](https://www.timesofisrael.com/housing-snapshot-october-2026-prices-continue-downward-trend)
- Baseline investor purchases, 2026: April 751 (−12% y/y, −43% m/m); May 1,319 (+43% y/y); June 1,392 (+64% y/y), with investor sales 1,487 (+40%) — Times of Israel monthly snapshots via search summary ([July 2026 snapshot](https://www.timesofisrael.com/housing-snapshot-july-2026-in-shadow-of-iran-war-home-prices-fall-0-3-in-march-april/), [Sept 2026 snapshot](https://www.timesofisrael.com/housing-snapshot-september-2026-prices-inch-upward-after-sharp-decline/)). October 2025 baseline: 759 investor purchases, the lowest since November 2023 — via [internationalinvestment.biz](https://internationalinvestment.biz/en/real-estate/6637-housing-in-israel-declining-sales-and-shrinking-investment.html)
- First-time buyers: JNS reports "3,907 first-home purchases in September, a 15% decline" — [JNS, "For now, Israel's housing market is at a standstill"](https://www.jns.org/for-now-israels-housing-market-is-at-a-standstill/). **Unverified and possibly a 2025 figure**: the article's open-market figure of 6,048 matches a September 2025 number elsewhere.

### Inferences
- Total volume is drifting down, but the mix is shifting. Developer incentives (20/80 and other deferred-payment schemes, discounts) kept new-home sales above last year, while Tel Aviv, the most expensive market, saw demand for new homes collapse in Q3.
- Investor demand rebounded in mid-2026 after the ceasefire, then faded again by August–September (−2% y/y in August, −29% y/y in September).

### Gaps
- No verified Finance Ministry Chief Economist release for September 2026 with total deals, the first-time-buyer share, or the new vs second-hand split. A September total-deals figure was not found.
- No October 2026 transaction data. These are normally published about mid-to-late November, possibly after the window.

## 3. Unsold new-home inventory

### Takeaway
Developers held a record-high **85,080 unsold new homes at end-September 2026**, according to CBS data published Nov 12, 2026. That equals nearly **26 months of sales** at the current pace. Jerusalem and Tel Aviv each hold more than 10,000 unsold homes. Inventory has stayed near 85,000–86,000 all year.

### Cited Findings
- 85,080 unsold new homes at end-September 2026, equal to about 26 months of sales; Jerusalem and Tel Aviv each have over 10,000 — [i24NEWS](https://www.i24news.tv/en/news/israel/economy/artc-israel-s-housing-sales-fall-as-unsold-new-homes-reach-85-000); [Globes Nov 12, 2026](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-new-home-sales-plunge-in-tel-aviv-1001563213)
- Nov 14, 2026 coverage (Israel Hayom via Israel National News): "more than 85,000 new homes remain unsold." The Housing Ministry director-general expects prices to fall further — [Israel National News flash](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/flashes/697419) (unverified snippet)
- Israel365 (Nov 1, 2026): record of about 85,000 unsold, another 217,000 homes under construction, about 2.5 years to clear at the current pace (attributed to CBS); about 46,000 in Tel Aviv and the Center and about 10,000 in Jerusalem — [Israel365 News](https://israel365news.com/422331/empty-homes-waiting-israels-record-housing-surplus-stands-ready-for-the-ingathering-of-the-exiles/). The estimate of about 2.5 years (about 30 months) differs from i24's 26 months. Israel365 is an advocacy-leaning outlet.
- Baseline: 86,090 at end-December 2025; about 86,290 at end-January 2026; about 85,680 at end-August 2026 — [Israel365](https://israel365news.com/422331/empty-homes-waiting-israels-record-housing-surplus-stands-ready-for-the-ingathering-of-the-exiles/), [Buyitinisrael](https://www.buyitinisrael.com/news/developers-are-winning-sellers-are-waiting) (search-summary attributions)
- Developer credit: bank credit to residential developers rose 40% in 2025 to about NIS 69 billion. At end-2025, 44% of projects financed by the five largest banks had construction running ahead of sales. The Bank of Israel calls the risk rising but "not yet immediate" — [Ynetnews, "Israel's housing market shows bubble warning signs as banks prop up developers" (c. May 2026)](https://www.ynetnews.com/real-estate/article/rjinoywkfx) (baseline, outside the window)

### Inferences
- Inventory is flat at a record level rather than climbing sharply: about 86,100 in Dec 2025, about 85,700 in Aug 2026 and about 85,100 in Sep 2026. Weak sales against a flat stock puts heavier pressure on developer pricing in Tel Aviv and the Center.

### Gaps

## 4. Bank of Israel: mortgages, mortgage rates and interest rate decisions

### Takeaway
At its **Oct 20–21, 2026** meeting, announced Oct 21, the Bank of Israel **held the policy rate at 3.25%**. Its notice cited new mortgage borrowing of about **NIS 10.3 billion (seasonally adjusted) in September 2026** and annual home prices down 1.8%. The bank's staff forecast puts the rate at 3.0% in about a year. The next decision is due **Nov 23, 2026** per the BoI notice; some calendars list Nov 26.

### Cited Findings
- Oct 21, 2026 decision: rate unchanged at 3.25%; discussions Oct 20–21; Research Department forecast of a 3% policy rate in about one year — [BoI, October 2026 decision PDF](https://www.boi.org.il/media/h1bl2ng2/october-2026.pdf); secondary confirmation that the rate was "held at 3.25% on 21 October" — [Kinyan Guide](https://kinyanguide.com/en/mortgage/mortgage-rates-israel/) / [reisr.com](https://reisr.com/interest-rates-in-israel/)
- Reasons for the hold (Calcalist): rising US Treasury yields and higher global rates complicate the outlook, a cut could weaken the shekel, and political risk — [Calcalist/Ctech, "Bank of Israel halts rate cuts as political and currency risks cloud outlook"](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/x1c9b5qbg)
- Prior cut (baseline): a secondary source says the BoI cut to 3.25% around Sept 1, 2026, taking prime to 4.75% — [Semerenko Group](https://semerenkogroup.com/bank-of-israel-rate-cut-3-75-percent-home-buyers-2026/) (secondary; the cut's date and size are unverified)
- New mortgages: about NIS 10.3 billion seasonally adjusted in September 2026 — [BoI October 2026 PDF](https://www.boi.org.il/media/h1bl2ng2/october-2026.pdf) (via search summary)
- Baseline: NIS 9.7 billion in May 2026, +19% y/y, partly from financing of deals signed about a year earlier — [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/business-and-innovation/banking-and-finance/article-900006); full-year 2025 about NIS 106 billion — [BoI Statistical Bulletin 2025](https://www.boi.org.il/media/3kefbdxu/2026-3-statistical-bulletin-pr-2025.pdf)
- Average mortgage rate: average unlinked mortgage rate 4.81% in Q2 2026, down from 4.89% in Q4 2025 — [Global Property Guide](https://www.globalpropertyguide.com/middle-east/israel/price-history) (secondary). A broker guide puts the weighted average across all tracks at about 4.5–5% in 2026 — [Kinyan Guide](https://kinyanguide.com/en/mortgage/mortgage-rates-israel/) (secondary; unverified)
- Next decision: BoI notice says Monday, Nov 23, 2026 — [BoI October 2026 PDF](https://www.boi.org.il/media/h1bl2ng2/october-2026.pdf). The BoI 2026 dates page and a third-party calendar reportedly list Nov 26 — [BoI dates page](https://www.boi.org.il/en/economic-roles/monetary-policy/interest-rate-announcement-dates-2026/); [Semerenko calendar](https://semerenkogroup.com/israel-2026-official-data-release-calendar-for-rates-and-inflation/). **Conflict; verify.**
- A Reuters/TradingView headline reads "Bank of Israel to hold rates after November cut." It appears to refer to an earlier year (2025), so it is **not** evidence of a November 2026 cut — [TradingView/Reuters](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2026:newsml_L8N3Y205P:0-bank-of-israel-to-hold-rates-after-november-cut/)

### Inferences
- Mortgage volume of about NIS 10.3 billion a month is running at or above 2025's monthly average (about NIS 8.8 billion). Developer-subsidised financing and payouts on older contracts are probably supporting it, since current sales are falling.
- September CPI came in lower than expected (1.6%), which leaves room for a cut at the November 23/26 meeting. The bank's own forecast of 3.0% in about a year implies only one more cut, so a hold is also plausible. This is speculative; no economists' poll for November was found.

## 5. Analyst and economist explanations and forecasts

### Takeaway
Commentators widely expect prices to keep falling gently. They cite high rates, record unsold inventory, weak sales (especially in Tel Aviv) and developers' hidden discounts. The Housing Ministry's director-general publicly expects further declines.

### Cited Findings
- The Housing Ministry director-general expects prices to fall further, with more than 85,000 new homes unsold (Nov 14, 2026) — [Israel National News](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/flashes/697419) (unverified snippet)
- Drivers cited for the decline: high interest rates, a record supply of unsold new housing, and high price levels — [JFeed](https://www.jfeed.com/business-and-economy/tmygeu)
- Globes expects prices to keep easing while sales stay weak; Tel Aviv supply of new homes rose about 5% over Q3 — via [Israel National News](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/436196)
- Developers are offering unpublished discounts, financing benefits and consumer-club deals rather than cutting list prices, so actual declines exceed the CBS figures — [Ynetnews, "As housing market slips into deep freeze, developers cut home prices – without saying they did"](https://www.ynetnews.com/real-estate/article/hjfiwsoymg); [Israel National News](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/flashes/697419)
- Bank of Israel Governor Amir Yaron urged developers to lower prices (c. September 2026, baseline) — [baba News](https://news.itsbaba.com/story/95043/bank-of-israel-governor-urges-developers-to-lower-housing-pr-107199) (secondary)
- Policy (baseline, date unclear): a government panel chaired by Avi Simhon ruled that banks financing projects may no longer restrict developer price cuts. Simhon estimated that removing the restrictions could cut prices about 10% within months — [Ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/real-estate/article/bksawbqveg); [Ynetnews, "Israel mulls bank reform to cut home prices amid glut of unsold apartments"](https://www.ynetnews.com/business/article/hyqqo7hdee). Implementation status unverified.
- Earlier forecast baseline: "Analysts see home price falls continuing into 2026" — [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-analysts-see-home-price-falls-continuing-into-2026-1001530109)

### Inferences
- The consensus is a slow nominal correction of roughly 1–3% a year, not a crash. Downside risks are developer liquidity (record inventory plus 40% growth in bank credit to developers) and the Simhon bank-restriction reform. Upside risks are further BoI cuts and post-war demand.

### Gaps
- No named bank-economist forecasts with numbers (e.g. Leumi, Hapoalim, Mizrahi) from within the window, and no Bloomberg/Reuters Israel-housing pieces from the window. Full-text access was blocked and the searches did not surface any.
