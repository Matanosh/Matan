## 1. Developer financing promotions (20/80, contractor loans, deferrals) and Bank of Israel / Supervisor of Banks action

### Takeaway
Developer financing incentives are still widespread and growing. About 30% of open-market new-home deals in August 2026 with delivery a year or more out carried a financing benefit, up from 20% in May. The Bank of Israel's March 2025 restrictions remain in force through end-2026. I found **no new BoI or Supervisor of Banks action** published Oct 16 – Nov 15, 2026, including no confirmed decision on extending the rules past 2026.

### Cited Findings
- The BoI directive, in force since March 2025 and running to end-2026, imposes extra capital allocation on bank-financed projects where at least 25% of units are sold on 20/80-style discount terms. It also caps developer-subsidized balloon loans at 10% of a bank's residential (monthly mortgage) credit activity. — [immobilier.co.il, "20/80: the Israeli payment plan that expires at the end of 2026"](https://www.immobilier.co.il/en/blog/20-80-the-israeli-payment-plan-that-expires-at-the-end-of-2026) (date unconfirmed); background: [Globes, "BoI moves to restrict developers' financing offers"](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-boi-moves-to-restrict-developers-financing-offers-1001505618) (2025, context); [Calcalist (Hebrew), "Bank of Israel decided: will try to cool contractors' 80-20 deals and balloon loans"](https://www.calcalist.co.il/real-estate/article/ry5511u6nkx) (2025, context)
- Practitioners report that true 20/80 plans are now rare and most projects have moved to 40/60 or 30/70 structures. — [immobilier.co.il](https://www.immobilier.co.il/en/blog/20-80-the-israeli-payment-plan-that-expires-at-the-end-of-2026) (date unconfirmed; marketing site, lower reliability)
- Calcalist reported that developers have found workarounds ("combinations") to bypass the BoI limits on financing promotions. — [Calcalist (Hebrew)](https://www.calcalist.co.il/real-estate/article/sjpvwxmwgl) (date unconfirmed, pre-window)
- Mako (Q3 2026) described new promotions in which developers pay buyers' rent until delivery ("the developers are already paying the rent"). It noted that the rules do not forbid benefits but make unlimited expansion of 20/80 and balloon deals harder. — [Mako (Hebrew)](https://www.mako.co.il/news-money/2026_q3/Article-8946b308def5f91026.htm) (about mid-2026; context, outside window)
- In August 2026, 30% of open-market deals for apartments delivered a year or more after signing included a financing benefit, up from 20% in May 2026. Developers sold 2,072 apartments on the open market in August, about 2% fewer y/y excluding subsidized sales. — [Times of Israel blog, "August: Developers sold 6% more, resale fell 7%"](https://blogs.timesofisrael.com/august-developers-sold-6-more-resale-fell-7/) (citing Calcalist/Bizportal; likely published mid/late Oct 2026, date unconfirmed)
- The BoI has warned that 80/20 promotions "postponed the mortgages, but not the risk." It argues that developer financing benefits widened the timing gap between purchase and mortgage drawdown, but says the gap is not a sign of developing risk in mortgage credit. — [Bizportal (Hebrew)](https://www.bizportal.co.il/general/news/article/20031890) (date unconfirmed)
- Bank credit to residential developers rose about 40% in 2025, to about NIS 69 billion (BoI data, via a Calcalist piece by Almog Azar). Banks have an incentive to keep developers afloat to avoid higher credit-loss provisions. — [Ynetnews/Calcalist, "Israel's housing market shows bubble warning signs as banks prop up developers"](https://www.ynetnews.com/real-estate/article/rjinoywkfx) (date unconfirmed)
- Monetary backdrop: the BoI Monetary Committee kept the policy rate unchanged at 3.25% on **October 21, 2026**. — [Bank of Israel press release, Oct 21, 2026](https://www.boi.org.il/en/communication-and-publications/press-releases/21-10-26ben/). Secondary sources say the rate was cut to 3.5% on July 6, 2026 and then to 3.25% in September. — [Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/news-money/2026_q3/Article-8946b308def5f91026.htm); [kinyanguide](https://kinyanguide.com/en/mortgage/mortgage-rates-israel/)

### Inferences
- The incentive share rose from 20% to 30% while the BoI caps stayed in place. That suggests developers are moving to alternative structures (30/70, 40/60, rent subsidies, price guarantees) instead of classic 20/80 plans.
- With the directive due to expire at end-2026, a BoI or Supervisor decision on extending, tightening or letting it lapse is a key item to watch in the coming weeks. No such decision appeared in the window.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm any Supervisor of Banks statement, circular or data release on developer promotions dated Oct 16 – Nov 15, 2026.
- I found no September or October 2026 figures on the share of deals with financing benefits.

## 2. Construction starts, completions, permits and the labor shortage

### Takeaway
Starts are easing but remain high: 17,866 units in Q2 2026, down about 10% y/y. Completions are lagging, and a record ~217,240 homes were under construction at end-June 2026. Labor is the binding constraint. In early November 2026 the Israel Builders Association lobbied against the government freeze on private recruitment of foreign workers from India and Sri Lanka, and the Housing Ministry is seeking about 20,000 more foreign workers. The industry estimates the shortage at 20,000–25,000 workers.

### Cited Findings
- Q2 2026 (Apr–Jun) dwelling starts were 17,866, down 10.3% from 19,911 in Q2 2025. Completions were 13,374, down 13.7% y/y. A record 217,240 homes were under active construction at end-June 2026. — [Semerenko Group, "Housing Starts Fall 7 Percent. Unfinished Flats Hit A Record 217,240"](https://semerenkogroup.com/housing-starts-fall-7-percent-unfinished-flats-hit-a-record-217240/) (date unconfirmed; secondary, citing CBS). CEIC lists 13,373 completions for Jun 2026 and seasonally adjusted starts of 19,976 for Jun 2026 vs 18,724 for Mar 2026. — [CEIC](https://www.ceicdata.com/en/israel/number-and-area-of-dwellings-completed-started-and-under-construction/dwellings-started-seasonally-adjusted)
- The Bank of Israel's October 2026 economic summary describes the Q2 decline in starts as "slight" and leaving them at a high level, with permits and completions also at high levels. — [BoI Office of the Spokesperson, October 2026 PDF](https://www.boi.org.il/media/h1bl2ng2/october-2026.pdf). Note that this conflicts in tone with the 10.3% y/y drop cited above. The BoI may be referring to the seasonally adjusted or q/q series.
- Q1 2026 starts were 18,896, versus 18,986 in Q4 2025. The 12-month period Apr 2025–Mar 2026 had about 76,470 starts against 62,140 completions. Starts peaked at about 81,020 in the 12 months to Sep 2025, with permits of about 81,560. — [Global Property Guide](https://www.globalpropertyguide.com/middle-east/israel/price-history) (context; secondary)
- Israel Builders Association Director General Igal Slovik wrote to several ministries asking them to reconsider halting direct private recruitment of construction workers from India and Sri Lanka. He warned of a gap between regulatory approval and actual arrivals. — [Ynetnews, "Israel builders warn of fresh housing delays after foreign worker recruitment freeze"](https://www.ynetnews.com/real-estate/article/sjrx1yyizl) (early Nov 2026, date unconfirmed); [Israel First TV Substack, Nov 9, 2026](https://israelfirsttvprogram.substack.com/p/monday-november-9-2026-israels-construction)
- The earlier freeze followed exhaustion of the 40,000-place quota for private hiring. Pending, unapproved applications will not be authorized for now, and the association asked for an additional quota under the same private model. — [Ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/real-estate/article/sjrx1yyizl); [Semerenko Group](https://semerenkogroup.com/israel-freezes-foreign-builder-hiring-homes-wait/)
- The Construction and Housing Ministry is seeking approval for an additional 20,000 foreign construction workers, warning that the labor shortage is delaying thousands of apartments and raising costs. This was reported around **Nov 2, 2026**. — [The Yeshiva World, "Building Bottleneck"](https://www.theyeshivaworld.com/news/israel-news/2618002/building-bottleneck-israel-seeks-20000-more-foreign-workers-as-construction-delays-mount.html)
- About 90,000 foreign construction workers are employed, and the industry still faces a shortage of about 20,000–25,000 workers. — [Israel First TV Substack, Nov 9, 2026](https://israelfirsttvprogram.substack.com/p/monday-november-9-2026-israels-construction). Other sources give about 73,000, or more than 80,000 (Builders Association); one later piece says 115,000. The total is **unsettled**.

### Inferences
- Construction duration is lengthening: starts exceed completions by about 14,000 a year, and a record pipeline is still under construction. Labor constraints and the recruitment freeze point to further delivery delays. That in turn raises developers' financing costs and the risk of late-delivery compensation claims.

### Gaps
- The CBS Q3 2026 (Jul–Sep) starts and completions release, normally due in mid/late November, was not found in the window.
- I found no current figures on Palestinian worker entry permits for construction. None of the sources I saw reported Palestinian workers returning in this window.
- I found no window-dated building-permit figures.

## 3. Listed developers: Q3 2026 results and announcements

### Takeaway
By Nov 15, 2026 I could not find published Q3 2026 financial reports for Azorim, Africa Israel Residences, Shikun & Binui, Electra, Ashtrom, Y.H. Dimri or Prashkovsky. These usually come out in late November. The latest company-level data is from the Q2 2026 reporting season (August 2026), shown below as context. Market-wide Q3 data shows new-home sales down 12% q/q, with Tel Aviv down 60%.

### Cited Findings
- **Market-wide Q3 2026 (CBS, published about Nov 12–13, 2026):** about 21,750 homes changed hands in Jul–Sep 2026, down 5.7% q/q and 7.3% y/y. New-home sales fell about 12% q/q but were about 4% above Q3 2025. Developers ended September with 85,080 unsold new homes, about 25.8–26 months of supply at the current pace. — [i24NEWS, "Israel's housing sales fall as unsold new homes reach 85,000"](https://www.i24news.tv/en/news/israel/economy/artc-israel-s-housing-sales-fall-as-unsold-new-homes-reach-85-000) (about Nov 13, 2026); [Globes, "New home sales plunge in Tel Aviv"](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-new-home-sales-plunge-in-tel-aviv-1001563213) (about Nov 13, 2026)
- New-home sales in Tel Aviv fell 60% in Q3 2026 versus Q2 and drove the national decline. Kiryat Gat's sales were largely government-subsidized. Haifa ranked first in total transactions for the first time in the CBS series. — [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-new-home-sales-plunge-in-tel-aviv-1001563213); [AgentPlus](https://agentplus-israel.com/en/news/new-home-sales-in-tel-aviv-drop-60-in-q3-haifa-tops-transaction-rankin-261112-c677) (Nov 12, 2026)
- CBS prices: July–August 2026 home prices were 1.8% lower than a year earlier. — [Israel National News](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/436196) (date unconfirmed, citing Calcalist/Globes)
- The CBS release of **Oct 14, 2026** reported about 24,200 homes sold in Jun–Aug 2026, up 8.1% y/y. About 35.8% of new apartments sold that summer (about 3,620 units) went through subsidized programs such as Mechir Matara. — [Buy It In Israel, "Developers Are Winning, Sellers Are Waiting"](https://www.buyitinisrael.com/news/developers-are-winning-sellers-are-waiting) (commentary, about mid/late Oct 2026). At end-August 2026, 85,680 new apartments were unsold. — [Times of Israel blog](https://blogs.timesofisrael.com/august-developers-sold-6-more-resale-fell-7/)
- **Context (Q2 2026 reports, August 2026):** Ashtrom Residential sold 52 apartments in Q2 2026 (vs 63). Shikun & Binui sold 44 (vs 74). Africa Israel Residences sold 71 in Q2 and 57 in the first 50 days of Q3. — [Globes, "Israeli home sales suffer summer slowdown"](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-israeli-home-sales-suffer-summer-slowdown-1001553592)
- **Context (Q2 2026):** Azorim had Q2 revenue from apartment sales of NIS 318 million (vs NIS 120 million in Q1, and below Q2 2025). It reported 15 contract cancellations worth NIS 48 million in H1 2026, mostly in Q1 and concentrated in Tel Aviv. — [baba News](https://news.itsbaba.com/story/85485/azorim-reports-15-contract-cancellations-amid-falling-apartm-95241). Israel Canada reported 4 cancelled agreements totalling NIS 15 million. — [WireIsrael](https://www.wireisrael.com/news/rapports-disrael-canada-quest-ce-qui-a-pes-sur-le-rsultat-net-et-combien-dappart-27)
- **Context (Q2 2026):** Y.H. Dimri sold 231 apartments in the quarter vs 168 a year earlier, but consideration grew much less, partly because of subsidized sales and promotions. Oron's residential arm sold only 6 units (vs 19). An aggregate review of Q2 developer reports found "37% more apartments, 5% more money". — [Klikat Nadlan (Hebrew)](https://klikatnadlan.co.il/q2gapreports2026/); [Klikat Nadlan – Oron](https://klikatnadlan.co.il/oronq22026/)
- Margins (context): Globes' review of 2025 reports found eroding profitability. In one example, a Rayk Aspen project's gross margin fell from 12.75% (2023) to 5.28% (2025). — [Globes, "Real estate companies report dwindling profits"](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-real-estate-companies-report-dwindling-profits-1001543483)
- A Hebrew search snippet attributes Q3 figures to Africa Israel Residences: 34 units sold for about NIS 116 million, 186 units in 9 months, revenue of NIS 209.8 million vs 257.5 million, and a gross margin of about 26%. **However, the result was dated about 350 days ago, so it is almost certainly Q3 2025, not Q3 2026. Do not use it as 2026 data.** — (search snippet only; source not verifiable)

### Inferences
- Developers are trading price (through promotions and subsidized tracks) for volume. Unit sales rose faster than revenue at Dimri, and the aggregate Q2 data shows units up 37% but money up only 5%. That points to margin pressure in the Q3 reports due late November.
- The Tel Aviv collapse (–60% q/q) suggests Tel Aviv-heavy developers (e.g., Azorim, Prashkovsky, Electra Residential) are the most exposed to weak Q3 sales and to cancellations.

### Gaps
- I found no Q3 2026 filings on maya.tase.co.il for the named companies in the window. These need to be checked after about Nov 20–30.
- I found no window-dated debt or net-financial-debt figures for any listed residential developer.

## 4. Land tenders (ILA / RMI) and Mechir Matara / Dira Behanacha lotteries

### Takeaway
ILA tender activity in the window that showed up in search was dominated by West Bank and East Jerusalem tenders: 732 units at Gva'ot, 91 at Oranit, and the delayed E1 opening set for Nov 19. I found no window-dated results for major tenders inside Israel proper. Subsidized programs remain a large share of new-home sales, about 36% this summer. I could not confirm any new Dira Behanacha lottery round opening in the window.

### Cited Findings
- E1 (1,234 units, tender 186/2026): the High Court rejected petitions against the Attorney General's decision to postpone opening the tender boxes to **Nov 19, 2026**, after the elections. The Oct 19 submission deadline stands. — [Peace Now](https://peacenow.org.il/en/e1-tender-box-opening-date); [Jewish News](https://www.jewishnews.co.uk/israel-delays-e1-settlement-tender-amid-election-concerns/)
- The ILA published tender 261/2026 for 732 residential units at Gva'ot near Bethlehem on Nov 8, 2026. — [Middle East Monitor, Nov 9, 2026](https://www.middleeastmonitor.com/20261109-israel-issues-tender-for-over-700-settlement-units-near-bethlehem-in-occupied-west-bank/); [WAFA](https://english.wafa.ps/Pages/Details/176782). Oranit tenders 224/2026 (86 units, published Nov 5) and 228/2026 (5 units, published Nov 3) were also issued. — [PalInfo, Nov 6, 2026](https://english.palinfo.com/news/2026/11/06/373208/); [WAFA](https://english.wafa.ps/Pages/Details/176685). These unit counts come from a Palestinian commission's reading of ILA records.
- At least 28 West Bank (including Jerusalem) land-marketing tenders were published in 2026 through Nov 8. 18 of them have a residential component, totalling 7,815 units. — [Peace Now](https://peacenow.org.il/en/e1-tender-box-opening-date)
- About 35.8% of new apartments sold in Jun–Aug 2026 (about 3,620) went through subsidized programs such as Mechir Matara (Target Price). Per the Finance Ministry, most of those sales came from Target Price projects. — [Buy It In Israel](https://www.buyitinisrael.com/news/developers-are-winning-sellers-are-waiting) (about Oct 2026)
- Context: about 16,000 new flats in Mechir Matara lotteries saw the reservist quota rise from 50% to 75% (August 2026). — [Semerenko Group](https://semerenkogroup.com/israel-promises-reservists-20000-discounted-homes/). The most recent documented Dira Behanacha round ran May 25–June 22, 2026, with 7,922 apartments in 82 project lotteries across 19 cities. — [Semerenko Group](https://semerenkogroup.com/when-is-israels-next-dira-behanacha-housing-lottery-updated-for-2025/) (context)
- Context: the number of land plots marketed for new housing has halved. — [International Investment](https://internationalinvestment.biz/en/real-estate/8877-number-of-land-plots-for-new-housing-in-israel-halves.html) (date unconfirmed)

### Inferences
- Subsidized-track sales (Mechir Matara, sometimes Dira Behanacha) are holding up developer volumes in the periphery (e.g., Kiryat Gat), while open-market sales are weak.

- I could not confirm whether a Dira Behanacha round opened or closed in Oct–Nov 2026; the official portal is dira.moch.gov.il.

## 5. Developer distress, bond issues and large project launches

### Takeaway
In this window I found no confirmed insolvency of a domestic Israeli residential developer and no major new residential bond issue. TASE real-estate distress news centred on US-based issuers. Banks are continuing to support developers through credit growth.

### Cited Findings
- Aya New York (Amir Shriki) closed a $104M Citibank refinancing and exited the TASE bond market. It cited weaker appetite for US real estate bonds and scrutiny of BVI-based borrowers. — [The Real Deal, Nov 9, 2026](https://therealdeal.com/new-york/2026/11/09/aya-new-york-lands-104m-loan-exits-israeli-bond-market/)
- Trustees for bondholders of the collapsed Sapir Corp. asked an Israeli court to pursue Alex Sapir personally for about $14M. — [The Real Deal, Nov 12, 2026](https://therealdeal.com/new-york/2026/11/12/alex-sapir-faces-14m-claim-from-israeli-bondholders/)
- Sanilevich Real Estate (a family firm) plans a NIS 200–250 million TASE bond debut based on its Q2 2026 financials. The article is from about late August 2026, so it predates the window, and the timing is unconfirmed. — [Ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/business/article/s1migbavfg)
- Context: residential developer bank credit grew about 40% in 2025, to about NIS 69 billion, and contractors held a record 83,400 unsold apartments at end-2025. — [Ynetnews/Calcalist](https://www.ynetnews.com/real-estate/article/rjinoywkfx)

### Inferences
- Unsold inventory is at about 26 months of supply. Starts are outpacing completions, labor is short and Tel Aviv sales have collapsed. Together these create elevated stress for smaller and mid-sized private developers, even though no listed failures surfaced in the window.

### Gaps
- I found no window-dated reports of specific Israeli residential developer bond issues, rating actions (Maalot/Midroog) or major project launches.
- I found no data on contractor insolvencies or receiverships in Oct–Nov 2026.
