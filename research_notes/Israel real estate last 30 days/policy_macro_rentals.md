# Israel Residential Real Estate: Policy, Taxation, Regulation, Macro and Rentals (Oct 16 – Nov 15, 2026)

Method note: these notes rest on web search results only. Direct page fetches failed because boi.org.il, calcalistech.com and timesofisrael.com were outside this session's allowed network hosts. Quoted figures come from the search engine's extracts of the cited pages, not from full reads. Where a publication date could not be confirmed, the item says so. Status labels: **[ENACTED/IN FORCE]**, **[PROPOSAL]**, **[DATA]**, **[FORECAST]**.

Overall context for the window: Israel held a Knesset election in late October 2026, about a week after the October 21 Bank of Israel decision. It produced a deadlock. As of mid-November there was no new government and no 2027 budget. Policy activity in the window was therefore mostly proposals and Finance Ministry staff work, not enacted measures.

## 1. Tax and policy changes or proposals: purchase tax, VAT, 2027 budget / Arrangements Law, urban renewal, Ministry of Housing

### Takeaway
No housing tax measure was enacted in the window, because no government or 2027 budget exists. The live items are Finance Ministry austerity proposals for a 2027–2028 two-year budget, the most relevant being repeal of the tax exemption on residential rental income, plus a VAT rate and tax-bracket freezes carried into 2027. There is also unresolved talk of lowering the 8% investor purchase tax, which by one account stays in force until at least February 10, 2027.

### Cited Findings
- **[PROPOSAL] 2027 budget not submitted.** The legal deadline for the government to submit a 2027 budget proposal to the Knesset passed on Sunday, November 1, 2026, and no new government had been formed. Calcalist says ending the year without an approved budget, as happened in 2020, "no longer seems far-fetched." The absolute deadline to pass a budget is March 31, 2027. — [Calcalist/Ctech, "Israel could go through all of 2027 without an approved budget"](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/z3g4nx8j4) (early Nov 2026); [Israel Investment Report, "Israel Has No 2027 Budget Yet"](https://israelinvestmentreport.substack.com/p/israel-has-no-2027-budget-yet-what)
- **[FORECAST] Interim budget.** The Bank of Israel's October 2026 staff forecast assumes 2027 begins with an interim (continuing) budget. — [Calcalist/Ctech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/z3g4nx8j4); [BoI Research Dept Staff Forecast, Oct 21, 2026](https://www.boi.org.il/en/communication-and-publications/press-releases/21-10-26-aen/)
- **[PROPOSAL] Finance Ministry austerity package for a 2027–2028 budget.** Despite the lack of a government, senior Finance Ministry officials are preparing a likely two-year 2027–2028 budget with austerity measures to cover heavy defense spending. The plan would keep in place through 2027 the freeze on income tax brackets, tax credit points and National Insurance adjustments, along with the current VAT rate, and add further revenue measures. — [Jerusalem Post, "Finance Ministry austerity plan revealed: Rent tax and tax credits for parents"](http://www.jpost.com/business-and-innovation/all-news/article-914689) (Nov 2026); [JPost, "Finance Ministry prepares plan to prevent increase in deficit…"](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912995) (Nov 2026)
- **[PROPOSAL] Rent-tax exemption repeal (landlords).** The Finance Ministry is examining cancelling the exemption on monthly residential rental income up to a ceiling of about NIS 5,600 and taxing rent "from the first shekel." JPost calls it one of the more practical proposals. Repeal would require Knesset legislation, opposition is expected, and it would be "a direct hit to the income of many apartment owners." — [Jerusalem Post](http://www.jpost.com/business-and-innovation/all-news/article-914689)
  - Background **[IN FORCE, older context]**: under current rules, residential rental income up to NIS 5,654/month (CPI-indexed, 2024 figure) is exempt with no reporting requirement. Income above that can be taxed at 10% under the alternative track. — [Knesset Research & Information Center, 2024](https://main.knesset.gov.il/EN/activity/mmm/Taxation110424.pdf)
- **[PROPOSAL / UNCERTAIN] Investor purchase tax (mas rechisha).** The Tax Authority chief signaled that purchase tax on investment apartments may fall. The report is inconsistent: it says the rate is set to drop to 6% in 2027, but also that it is expected to return to 5% unless a different decision is made. He framed the tax as a housing-policy tool rather than a fiscal one. The publication date was not confirmed, so this may predate the window. — [Buy It In Israel, "Tax Authority Chief Signals Reduction in Purchase Tax"](https://www.buyitinisrael.com/news/possible-reduction-in-purchase-tax-on-investment-properties)
- **[IN FORCE] 8% investor purchase tax extended by election provisions.** Election-related provisions automatically extend certain measures, including the 8% purchase tax on real estate investors, until February 10, 2027. If they are not extended, the brackets could change. — [Calcalist/Ctech, "Political paralysis could cost Israel billions…"](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rexnr6xcn)
- **[IN FORCE / CONTEXT] Purchase tax bracket freeze.** A draft provision freezes indexation of purchase-tax brackets for 2025–2027, with brackets updated in 2028 using the January 2027 index. Secondary guides disagree on whether investor brackets are frozen to Dec 31, 2026 or through 2027. — [Semerenko Group (commercial guide)](https://semerenkogroup.com/israel-purchase-tax-freeze-2025-2027-how-it-really-reshapes-the-cost-of-buying-a-home/)
- **[CONTEXT] Purchase tax and the Compensation Fund.** The share of purchase-tax revenue directed to the Property Tax Compensation Fund was cut to 25% in 2026. A Tax Authority official said that for 2027 it will be necessary to reconsider directing 75% or even 100% to the fund. — [Calcalist/Ctech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rexnr6xcn) (date unconfirmed)
- **[CAMPAIGN PROPOSAL] Long-term rental housing.** During the election campaign, the Bennett–Lapid joint list pledged 100,000 long-term rental apartments within a decade, 40,000 of them about 30% below market. — [Buy It In Israel, "Election Day: What It Means for Israeli Real Estate"](https://www.buyitinisrael.com/news/election-day-what-it-means-for-israeli-real-estate)
- **[IN FORCE, context] Bank of Israel restrictions on developer financing.** The BoI imposed restrictions in March 2025: a 150% capital allocation where more than 40% of the price is deferred, and developer-subsidized balloon loans capped at 10% of mortgage originations. About 10 days before the search date (~early Nov 2026), Globes reported developers still find workarounds. Gindi and Aviv Melisron launched new high-end sales offers, about 85,000 new apartments await buyers, and Finance Ministry data showed financing incentives again at about 30% of new-apartment sales in August, higher than before the restrictions. — [Globes, "Developers' finance schemes find ways around BoI restrictions"](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-developers-finance-schemes-find-ways-around-boi-restrictions-1001561999)
- **[DATA] Financing incentives in September.** A Globes report says the share of new-home deals with a financing incentive reached 45% in September 2026. Tel Aviv leads the stagnation, with only 537 new units sold in Q3 2026. — [Globes, "New home sales plunge in Tel Aviv"](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-new-home-sales-plunge-in-tel-aviv-1001563213) (Oct/Nov 2026; exact date unconfirmed). The 30% (August) and 45% (September) figures come from different articles and may use different definitions.
- **[CONTEXT] Urban renewal.** No new TAMA 38 or pinui-binui rule change in the window turned up. Secondary sources describe TAMA 38 as closed to new permits: national cutoff Aug 29, 2024, and extension cities May 18, 2026. Pinui-binui, with a two-thirds owner majority for 4+ unit buildings, and the municipal "Shaked" track are now the main routes. — [Semerenko Group, "TAMA 38 Israel: What Ended In 2024 & What's Next"](https://semerenkogroup.com/tama-38-israel/); [Semerenko, Urban renewal](https://semerenkogroup.com/urban-renewal/)

### Inferences
- With no government, no 2027 budget and no Arrangements Law, the default for early 2027 is an interim budget with existing tax settings carried over: 8% investor purchase tax, frozen brackets, current VAT. Any purchase-tax cut for investors or rent-tax change depends on a new coalition passing a budget, most likely in Q1 2027 against the March 31 deadline.
- Repealing the rental-income exemption would lower net yields for small landlords and could pass through partly to rents. It is a proposal only, and JPost flags political resistance.
- Developer financing incentives still run at 30–45% of new-home deals despite BoI curbs. That signals price pressure in the new-build segment, and regulators may move to tighten again.

### Gaps
- No Ministry of Construction and Housing announcement (Dira Behanacha / Dira Bemechir Lemishtaken lotteries, new tenders) from Oct 16 – Nov 15, 2026 turned up in search.
- No confirmed date for the Tax Authority chief's purchase-tax remarks, so the 5% vs 6% vs 8% investor-rate outlook for 2027 is unresolved.
- Primary pages (gov.il, Knesset committees, Tax Authority) could not be fetched in this session.

## 2. Bank of Israel rate decision, inflation, shekel, and stated housing impact

### Takeaway
On October 21, 2026, the BoI held its policy rate at 3.25%, ending three straight cuts (prime 4.75%). It cited higher global yields, shekel weakness and pre-election uncertainty. Its statement put home prices down 1.8% year on year and new-tenant rents up 5.3%. The next decision is November 23, 2026, outside the window. September CPI was 1.6% year on year; October CPI was due November 15 and was not found.

### Cited Findings
- **[ENACTED] Rate hold.** "The Monetary Committee decides on October 21, 2026 to keep the interest rate unchanged at 3.25 percent." — [Bank of Israel press release, Oct 21, 2026](https://www.boi.org.il/en/communication-and-publications/press-releases/21-10-26ben/); [Reuters via US News, "Bank of Israel Holds Interest Rates Steady a Week Before Election," Oct 21, 2026](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-10-21/bank-of-israel-holds-interest-rates-steady-a-week-before-election)
- The hold ended a run of three consecutive cuts. Earlier in 2026: 4% in January, a cut in May, 3.50% on July 6 and 3.25% on September 1. Analysts cited rising US Treasury yields and higher global rates, and noted a cut could pressure the shekel. — [Calcalist/Ctech, "Bank of Israel halts rate cuts as political and currency risks cloud outlook"](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/x1c9b5qbg) (Oct 21, 2026); [Semerenko Group](https://semerenkogroup.com/bank-of-israel-rate-cut-3-75-percent-home-buyers-2026/)

### Inferences
- With inflation at 1.6% and housing prices falling, the BoI's pause looks driven by external factors (global yields, oil near $110, shekel weakness, election risk) rather than housing. If a government forms and the shekel steadies, the market will price a resumed cut on Nov 23 or in early 2027.
- The gap between falling sale prices (-1.8% YoY) and rising rents (+3.8% housing CPI; +5.3% new-tenant rents) lifts gross rental yields for investors. The 8% purchase tax and a possible rent-tax repeal offset part of that.

## 3. Security, geopolitical and economic factors cited as affecting housing demand

### Takeaway
The dominant factor in the window was political: the late-October election deadlock froze buyer decisions, hit real estate and construction stocks hard, and blocked the 2027 budget. Credit ratings held: S&P A/stable, reaffirmed around Nov 8. Southern (Tkuma) reconstruction is advanced and housing there is scarce; the north still lacks a multi-year framework.

### Cited Findings
- **[DATA] Election and markets.** Preliminary results pointed to deadlock and possibly a narrow government or fresh elections. Reports of the post-election drop vary: the TA real estate index fell 3.66% (Ynet), real estate and insurance indices fell more than 6% (JPost), and one report puts the construction index down about 8%. — [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/business/article/skl084kpgx); [JPost](https://www.jpost.com/business-and-innovation/article-913120); [Calcalist/Ctech, "Israeli stocks plunge as election results cloud path to new government"](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/hkzfchjamg)
- **Government formation.** President Herzog tasked Gadi Eisenkot (Yashar party) with forming a government (around Nov 1, 2026). He has 28 days plus up to 14 days of extensions, and parties that recommended him had not agreed to govern together. — [Al Jazeera, Nov 1, 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/1/israel-election-when-will-government-be-formed-who-will-lead-it); [MEPC](https://mepc.org/principals-brief/how-israel-forms-a-government-coalition-scenarios-and-the-timeline-ahead). A report of a "December 6 deadline" triggering May elections looks unreliable and conflicts with the Basic Law timelines. — [news-articles.net](https://politics-government.news-articles.net/content/2026/11/08/israel-s-december-6-deadline-for-government-formation.html)
- **Buyer behavior.** Many buyers deferred decisions until after Election Day. The base case is a slow market through coalition talks, with activity picking up once a government is sworn in and a budget passes. — [Buy It In Israel, "Election Day: What It Means for Israeli Real Estate"](https://www.buyitinisrael.com/news/election-day-what-it-means-for-israeli-real-estate)
- **Political paralysis cost.** Ministries face a budget freeze, infrastructure tenders may be postponed, and tax revenues are at risk. — [Calcalist/Ctech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rexnr6xcn)
- **[DATA] Credit ratings.** S&P kept Israel at A with a stable outlook after its semiannual review (JNS, Nov 8, 2026), two notches below the prewar AA-. Moody's is at Baa1/stable and does not expect the election to have an immediate rating impact. ## 4. Rental market: CBS rent index and trends in Tel Aviv, center and periphery

### Takeaway
Rents keep rising while sale prices fall. The CBS rent index rose 0.4% in September, the housing CPI component is up 3.8% year on year, and new-tenant rents are up about 4.7–5.3%. Tel Aviv remains the most expensive and fastest-rising market; the northern district is cheapest. No October CBS rent reading was found.

### Cited Findings

- **[DATA]** Housing (rent) component of CPI: +3.8% YoY, unchanged in September. — [BoI press release, Oct 21, 2026](https://www.boi.org.il/en/communication-and-publications/press-releases/21-10-26ben/)
- **[DATA]** New-tenant contract rents: +5.3% over 12 months (BoI, Oct 21). — [Jewish Link](https://jewishlink.news/israels-housing-market-during-the-iran-conflict-have-property-prices-really-fallen-enough/). An earlier CBS-based figure to July 2026: renewing tenants +2.6%, new tenants +4.7%. — [Times of Israel, Housing snapshot October 2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/housing-snapshot-october-2026-prices-continue-downward-trend). The two figures differ, probably because of different reference periods.
- **[DATA, context Q1 2026]** CBS (via ynet): average rent +0.7% QoQ and +3.5% YoY in Jan–Mar; renewing tenants +2.2%. Neighborhood medians range from ₪4,000 (HaTikva) to ₪21,500 (Kochav HaTzafon). — [yaeli.ai listings analysis](https://yaeli.ai/en/rental-prices) (aggregator, lower reliability)

### Inferences
- Rents rising about 4–5% on new leases while prices fall about 2% widens gross yields. That partly explains why the Finance Ministry sees the rental-income exemption as a revenue target.
- The Bennett–Lapid long-term rental pledge (100k units, 40k below market) would add supply in the medium term only if a government including them forms.
