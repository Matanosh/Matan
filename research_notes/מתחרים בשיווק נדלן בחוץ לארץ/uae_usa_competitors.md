# Israeli / Hebrew-language competitors marketing UAE (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, RAK) and US (Miami/Florida) real estate to Israeli investors

> **Method note (read first):** Research date 2026-11-16. Every competitor website fetch (shalom-dubai.com, skyhigh-dubai.com, mayors.co.il, dubai-invest.co.il, realogroup.com, miami-invest.co.il, israelhayom.co.il) failed. Those hosts are outside this session's allowed network, so the pages could not be opened. Findings below come from search results: page titles, URLs and search-engine summaries of those pages. That means:
> (a) Site-UX items (hero text, calculators, WhatsApp buttons, payment-plan widgets, videos, EN/HE toggles) are **mostly unverified**.
> (b) Claims are attributed to the URL the search engine summarised.
> (c) Paywalled press (Globes, TheMarker, Calcalist) was available as summaries only.
> Before anyone relies on these profiles, someone should open each site in a browser and confirm the details.

## 1. Who are the most visible competitors? (name, URL, markets, developers, years, offices)

### Takeaway
The Hebrew search results for Dubai are crowded with Israeli "accompaniment" brands (ליווי). Most are content-heavy sites built on 2025–2026 guides. Few visibly cover RAK, and almost none combine UAE and US the way MS Group Global does. US players are a separate group: buy-renovate-rent operators in Florida and the Midwest, Miami brokers, and syndication/fund models. Pre-construction condo marketers selling Miami to Israelis barely show up in Hebrew search.

## 2. Positioning, promise and target audience

### Takeaway
The dominant Hebrew promise is "full accompaniment for Israelis" (ליווי מלא), sold through education: dated "2025/2026 guides", FAQs, and tax and visa explainers. The US players instead lead with a low ticket and a yield number ("from $50K", "6–8%").

### Cited Findings
- Mayors: "Dubai real-estate investments for Israelis **with full accompaniment**". — [mayors.co.il](https://mayors.co.il/)
- Realo Group: "where your money works". — [realogroup.com](https://realogroup.com/)
- Shalom Dubai: positions itself as "the leading company" for buying and brokering. — [shalom-dubai.com](https://shalom-dubai.com/)
- Sky High: a "2026 guide for the Israeli investor" plus "5 reasons to buy with professional accompaniment", an education-led funnel. — [skyhigh-dubai.com](https://skyhigh-dubai.com/dubai-real-estate-professional/)
- Pacific Holdings: "safe yield" with entry from $50,000, aimed at the mass-affluent Israeli investor. — [pacificholdings.co.il](https://www.pacificholdings.co.il/)
- Barak Tzur: 6–8% yield, $100K minimum, Lehigh Acres. — [baraktzur.com](https://baraktzur.com/florida-investments/)
- Common Dubai selling points repeated across Israeli sites:
  - Israelis may buy freehold since the 2020 Abraham Accords.
  - 4% DLD fee.
  - Investor visa from AED 750,000 (two-year visa).
  - Gross yields quoted anywhere from 4–6% to 6–10%.
  — [dubai-invest summary](https://www.dubai-invest.co.il/); [israel-dubai](https://israel-dubai.co.il/property-in-dubai/)

### Inferences
- Target audience: Israeli private investors with roughly $100K–$600K to deploy. Dubai brands lean toward off-plan buyers; US brands lean toward "turn-key rental" buyers.
- The education-heavy SEO model means MS Group Global will compete for search visibility with these guide clusters, not only with brand names.

### Gaps
- Target-audience segmentation (age, ticket size) is not stated in the evidence. The inference above is based on stated minimums only.

## 7. Good practice vs. questionable practice

### Takeaway
Some Israeli competitors openly warn against "guaranteed yield". Others lead with "safe yield", double-digit yield headlines, or speculative appreciation claims tied to the Wynn casino in RAK. These are the clearest red flags in the category.

### Cited Findings
**Good practice**

### Inferences
- An "honest-numbers" stance would differentiate MS Group Global from the "safe yield" and "act now" players and align with the more credible ones (Realo, Dubai Team). That stance means net-yield calculators, no guarantees, a RERA/DLD-verifiable licence, escrow explained, and an Israeli-tax disclaimer.
- RAK is in a hype phase in Hebrew marketing. Projects like the client's RAK portfolio benefit from Wynn interest but carry reputational risk if marketed with appreciation promises.
