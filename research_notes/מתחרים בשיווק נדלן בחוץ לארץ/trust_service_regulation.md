# Trust, After-Sale Service and Regulatory Boundaries for Overseas Real-Estate Marketing to Israeli Private Investors

Research date: 2026-11-16. Method note: direct page fetches of bizportal.co.il and globes.co.il were blocked in this session (host not in the network allowlist), so the findings below rest on search-engine summaries of the cited pages. Paywalled articles (TheMarker, Globes premium) were read only through those summaries. Treat exact wording and figures as "per the cited article's summary" and confirm against the original before publishing client-facing copy.

## 1. Israel Securities Authority (רשות ניירות ערך): warnings, when a property deal becomes a securities offering, and red flags

### Takeaway
The ISA's long-standing position is that a "unit" abroad (especially a hotel or serviced unit) sold for a passive return can be a security even when it is registered in the buyer's name. Selling it to more than 35 non-qualified investors without a prospectus breaks the law. The 15 October 2026 warning is about foreign investment and trading websites written in Hebrew. It is not specifically about real estate, but its red flags apply directly to overseas-property marketing.

### Cited Findings
**The 15 Oct 2026 warning (foreign websites in Hebrew)**
- On 15 October 2026 the ISA warned the public about foreign investment and trading websites that present themselves in Hebrew although they are not supervised in Israel, and sometimes have no link to Israel at all. — [TheMarker, 2026-10-15](https://www.themarker.com/markets/2026-10-15/ty-article/.premium/000001a1-3ea2-d253-a9e1-feaa30ea0000); [Funder](https://www.funder.co.il/article/211913); [Walla Finance](https://finance.walla.co.il/item/3872283)
- The ISA's core message: a website being in Hebrew does not show that its operator is Israeli, licensed or supervised in Israel. It said automatic translation and AI let international sites address investors in dozens of languages. — [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/general/news/article/20044833); [Kipa](https://www.kipa.co.il/%D7%9B%D7%A1%D7%A3/1233773-0/)
- Red flags the ISA listed include: no licence or no recognised regulator; Hebrew that looks machine-translated; unusual domains such as .top or .vip; and demands to deposit money into a foreign account or in crypto. — [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/general/news/article/20044833); [Bhol](https://www.bhol.co.il/news/1741858)
- The ISA warned that dealing with bodies not supervised in Israel can make it hard to enforce rights and recover money. It urged investors to check the operating entity's identity, country of registration and regulatory status before giving personal details or transferring money. The ISA's website lets the public check whether a body is supervised. — [Funder](https://www.funder.co.il/article/211913); [Walla Finance](https://finance.walla.co.il/item/3872283)
- Date discrepancy: one secondary source cites an ISA item titled "Multilingual websites in investment and trading (October 2026)" dated 30.10.2026. This may be the full document or an update of the 15 Oct warning; I did not verify it. — [Hamirpeset Mag](https://www.hamirpesetmag.com/guides/investment-scam-red-flags)
- The ISA issued an earlier warning in October 2025 about foreign sites posing as Israeli and offering AI-based stock forecasts. — [Gornitzky](https://www.gornitzky.co.il/%D7%A8%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A2-%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%90%D7%96%D7%94%D7%A8%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%91%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%92%D7%A2-%D7%9C%D7%90%D7%AA%D7%A8/)

**When an overseas property deal becomes a securities offering**
- ISA audit findings stated that marketing a unit abroad, or a hotel unit expected to produce passive returns, is an investment in securities even if the unit is registered in the land registry (טאבו) in the investor's name. (The news coverage is roughly 2021–2022.) — [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/realestates/news/article/801811); [Walla Finance](https://finance.walla.co.il/item/3489971)
- The ISA staff look at the economic substance: a financial, passive investment made with an expectation of return. In the hotel-unit case examined, each investor bought a specific registered unit, but the agreements entitled investors to a share of the whole hotel's profits in proportion to their holdings, regardless of how their own unit performed. The staff said this tie to the project's overall activity strengthens the need for securities-law protection. — [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/realestates/news/article/801811); ISA staff position document [OFFERING.pdf](https://www.new.isa.gov.il/images/Fittings/isa/asset_library_pic/OFFERING.pdf) (text extraction garbled; exact wording not verified)
- An ISA audit report found that two overseas real-estate ventures it examined had allegedly breached the Securities Law by offering securities without a prospectus. The ISA also noted that a company may offer securities without a prospectus to no more than 35 investors. — summarised from [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/realestates/news/article/801811) and [Ronen Adini law office](https://www.ronenadini.co.il/articles/%D7%A8%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9A-%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%91%D7%94-%D7%92%D7%91%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%92%D7%99%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%94/); ISA guidance on [private offerings](https://www.new.isa.gov.il/images/Fittings/isa/asset_library_pic/al_lobby/al_lobby-6253f4f00241c/PrivateOfferings.pdf)
- An ISA audit report held that a financial investment in a property project, even with land-registry registration, can be a security, and that a guaranteed return may not be promised to investors. — [Bizzness](https://bizzness.net/%D7%A8%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A2-%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A2%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%98%D7%95%D7%97%D7%94-%D7%90%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%91%D7%98%D7%99%D7%97/)
- Under ISA chair Anat Guetta, the ISA sharply criticised the marketing conduct of investment vehicles raising money from the public, mostly real-estate corporations offering unlisted securities. The Globes headline was "ISA: US real-estate investment companies mislead the public" (c. 2019, paywalled). — [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001289549)
- In one enforcement example, a company that advertised a 22% annual return received a warning to stop activity on suspicion of breaching the Securities Law. This was not a US real-estate case. — [Ice](https://www.ice.co.il/law/news/article/1059547)
- The ISA's tools include warning the public and banning publication of expected returns on unregulated investments without a prospectus. — [Wikipedia: Israel Securities Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Securities_Authority)
- In 2026 the ISA published draft directives on "one-off services and alternative/restricted assets": the first in late April 2026, the second on 10 September 2026. They aim to reduce information gaps and conflicts of interest that in some cases led to enforcement. The search summary does not say whether they cover real estate. — [Barnea law firm updates](https://barlaw.co.il/client_updates/)

### Inferences
- In practice, the line falls between selling a specific unit the buyer owns and controls (title, own lease, own income) and selling a share in a pooled return (hotel profit pools, "investment clubs", guaranteed yield, developer-managed rental pools paying a fixed percentage). For MS Group Global, guaranteed-yield or rental-pool products in hotels or serviced apartments (common in Georgia/Batumi and UAE) are the highest-risk category under ISA criteria.
- Advertising a specific return figure (e.g. "8% guaranteed") is both an ISA red flag and a likely consumer-protection issue (see section 3). Compliant copy should frame yields as historical or illustrative, unguaranteed and sourced.
- The Hebrew-website warning raises the trust bar. A visibly Israeli legal entity (company number, Israeli address, named officers, local regulator references) separates MS Group from the offshore sites the ISA describes.

### Gaps
- I could not read the ISA's own Hebrew press release for 15.10.2026 or confirm the 30.10.2026 document; isa.gov.il pages did not come up as fetchable.
- I found no ISA statement in 2025–2026 specific to Georgia, Portugal, Romania, Ukraine, Cyprus or UAE property marketing.
- The exact text of the ISA staff position on real-estate offerings (OFFERING.pdf) could not be verified.

## 2. Israel Tax Authority basics that good competitors explain

### Takeaway
Israeli residents must report foreign rental income. They can choose a flat 15% track (section 122A) or the regular marginal track with expenses and a foreign-tax credit under the relevant treaty. Better competitors publish Hebrew guides on this, often per country (for example an Israel–Georgia treaty guide).

### Cited Findings
- Section 122A of the Income Tax Ordinance lets an individual pay 15% tax on rental income from property outside Israel instead of the regular rates. — [Arzi Hiba Cohen tax newsletter](https://www.ahec-tax.co.il/newsletters/%D7%9E%D7%91%D7%96%D7%A7-%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A8-660/15-%D7%A2%D7%93-%D7%94%D7%91%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%A2%D7%9C-%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9C/); [din.co.il](https://www.din.co.il/articles/4211/B-22639/)
- On the 15% track the tax is on gross income, only depreciation is deductible, and no credit is given for foreign tax paid. The regular track taxes at marginal rates but allows expenses (mortgage interest, management, repairs, insurance, depreciation) and a foreign-tax credit. — [Dray & Dray CPA](https://cpa-dray.com/he/blog/%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%A0%D7%93%D7%9C%D7%9F-%D7%91%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9C/); [Yuvalim](https://www.yuvalim-ins.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94-%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%94-%D7%9E%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9C/)
- Under the 15% track the income can be reported and paid without filing a full return (file type 95). A tax return is required when foreign rental income exceeds ₪375,000 a year. The threshold comes from a secondary source and should be checked against the current Tax Authority figure. — [Efraty](https://www.efraty.com/%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2-%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%99/%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94/%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94-2020/%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94-2013-2017/%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%90%D7%A8%D7%A5/%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9C/)
- Through tax treaties and the foreign-tax-credit mechanism, tax paid in the property's country is offset against Israeli tax. For example, a US investor files a US return and then the Israeli annual return (form 1301). — [Project X Club magazine](https://www.projectxclub.com/magazine/%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%A2-%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%A4%D7%9C-%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%A2%D7%9C-%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%A7%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A0%D7%93%D7%9C%D7%9F-%D7%91%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9C/)
- A 2026 CPA guide covers what an Israeli resident must report on foreign income and assets in 2026. — [ZCPA](https://zcpa.co.il/Article/foreign-income-assets-reporting-2026)
- Sale of a foreign residential apartment has its own tax treatment, covered in CPA guides. — [BSH CPA](https://www.bshcpa.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%92%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9C/)
- Competitor examples that publish tax content: Elson Group has a Hebrew guide to the Israel–Georgia tax treaty — [Elson Group](https://elsongroup.co.il/amanat-mas-israel-georgia-madrich/). Nadlan B'Jeans and Nadlan Global advertise international tax planning (Greece, Cyprus, Portugal) — [rej.co.il](https://rej.co.il/); [nadlan-global.co.il](https://nadlan-global.co.il/). Project X publishes a double-taxation article — [Project X](https://www.projectxclub.com/magazine/%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%A2-%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%A4%D7%9C-%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%A2%D7%9C-%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%A7%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A0%D7%93%D7%9C%D7%9F-%D7%91%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9C/).

### Inferences
- A best-practice tax page for MS Group Global would cover: the 15% versus regular track choice and when each pays off; the reporting duty; treaty status per country (Georgia, Portugal, Romania, Ukraine, Cyprus, UAE, USA); and capital-gains treatment on exit. It should defer to an independent Israeli CPA rather than give individual tax advice.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve primary Israel Tax Authority pages (gov.il) or a current list of which target countries have treaties with Israel. UAE treaty status in particular should be checked against official sources.
- I did not confirm the current reporting thresholds for foreign assets.

## 3. Consumer protection, misleading advertising and broker licensing

### Takeaway
Israeli consumer-protection law punishes misleading advertising with administrative fines that accumulate per violation, and the Consumer Protection Authority has fined real-estate marketers repeatedly. The Brokers Law requires a licence for real-estate brokerage in Israel. I found no source that says clearly whether it covers brokering foreign property marketed from Israel.

### Cited Findings
- Misleading-advertising breaches are classed as severe violations, with an expected administrative fine of ₪45,000 per violation, and fines can accumulate. The Consumer Protection Authority announced an intended fine of about ₪1.9 million on Gindi Holdings over a misleading campaign for a property project. — [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001029226)
- The authority fined Eldad Perry Real Estate ₪648,000 for misleading consumers in ads for a Bat Yam project. — [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/article/818569)
- The authority imposed fines totalling ₪2,357,600 on 17 companies marketing agricultural land. — [Walla Nadlan](https://nadlan.walla.co.il/item/3449003); [Davar](https://www.davar1.co.il/321362/)
- Procedure: the regulator sends notice of intent to fine; the company has 45 days to respond; decisions are published on the authority's site (four years for a corporation). Fines can be challenged in court; a magistrates' court cancelled one fine in an unrelated case. — [Consumer Protection Law, Nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/70305.htm); [Chamber of Commerce](https://www.chamber.org.il/serviceslobby/legal/1857/70210/)
- Liability for a misleading ad can extend to whoever distributed it or decided to publish it, if the ad was misleading on its face or they knew it was. — [Mishpati / DS Legal summaries](https://ds-legal.co.il/%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%A4%D7%98%D7%99-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9B%D7%99-%D7%A0%D7%93%D7%9C%D7%9F/)
- Brokerage in Israel requires passing the Registrar of Brokers' licensing exam (Ministry of Justice). It is forbidden to advertise oneself as a broker without a licence, and every ad must show the broker's details and licence number. — [Mishpati](https://www.mishpati.co.il/article/721); [Wikipedia: תיווך נדל"ן](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9A_%D7%A0%D7%93%D7%9C%22%D7%9F)
- New broker regulations (recent reform) ban "seriousness deposits" (דמי רצינות), require full transparency, and bar advertising a property without the owner's consent. — [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/bkkpzcliyl); [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001503869)
- A broker who breaks the law faces up to ₪200,000 in fines, up to a year in prison, and suspension or loss of licence. This comes from a law-firm guide. — [DS Legal](https://ds-legal.co.il/%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%A4%D7%98%D7%99-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9B%D7%99-%D7%A0%D7%93%D7%9C%D7%9F/)
- The Consumer Protection Authority caught 101 unlicensed real-estate brokers in one enforcement action. — [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001135276)

### Inferences
- MS Group Global's ads (social, Hebrew landing pages, WhatsApp) are exposed both to consumer-protection rules (yield and price claims, "starting from" prices, completion dates, "guaranteed") and to ISA rules on promising returns. One compliance review of yield and return wording would cover both.
- Showing an Israeli broker licence number, if the group holds one, would be a cheap trust signal. Whether a licence is legally required for foreign property is unresolved.

### Gaps
- I found no Consumer Protection Authority fine aimed specifically at an overseas-property marketer, and no ruling on whether the Brokers Law covers foreign property. A local lawyer should confirm.

## 4. Known collapses and scandals, and what investors now look for

### Takeaway
The main Israeli cases are MHR (Germany), SDB, Velora, HAP and Vision and Beyond (USA/UK), mostly in 2022–2025, as rising interest rates exposed leveraged, prospectus-free, pooled structures. Investors and advisers now ask for escrow or trust accounts, bank or developer guarantees, independent local lawyers, verified title registration and a developer track record.

### Cited Findings
**MHR 1 (Germany)**
- MHR, owned by Tzach Hermon and Yigal Manovich, raised hundreds of millions of shekels from hundreds of Israeli investors for German residential and commercial property. It stopped paying ongoing returns. Liquidation petitions were rejected in the District Court and the Supreme Court. — [Nadlan Center](https://www.nadlancenter.co.il/article/13406); [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/article/s1idophu1l)
- The ISA opened a criminal investigation in February 2023. In January 2025 it passed the file to the State Attorney to consider charges of unlawful public offering without a prospectus, fraud and money laundering, with suspected sums over ₪200 million. — [TheMarker, 2025-01-07](https://www.themarker.com/markets/2025-01-07/ty-article/.premium/00000194-4096-dfbb-a7bc-6eb68b0f0000); [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/capitalmarket/news/article/20012160); [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001498742)
- A later report says the case is at the pre-indictment hearing stage and the suspicions have narrowed significantly. I could not confirm whether an indictment was filed. — [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/realestates/news/article/20048339)
- A court approved a settlement for MHR 1's hundreds of investors. About 400 investors had sued in the Tel Aviv Economic Court in February 2025, and the settlement pays them tens of millions of shekels. — [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/realestates/news/article/20048339); [Nadlan Center](https://www.nadlancenter.co.il/article/15824)

**USA and UK**
- HAP Investment (New York) said its "HAP 7" project failed, and about $70 million invested by hundreds of Israelis was lost. — [Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/finances-real-estate/Article-e2f00a44e67f691026.htm)
- Vision and Beyond, which invested in US multifamily property, closed and its owners disappeared, leaving dozens of investors with large debts. — [Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/finances-money/Article-4c29fa217520491027.htm)
- Several SDB projects went into insolvency, receivership or cash-flow distress and were sold at losses of tens of percent; investors lost tens of millions of shekels. US banks foreclosed three Velora projects worth about $28 million. — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/real-estate/article/hjkyhapsze); [Calcalist Tech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/hkghdrdogg)
- UK developer BYM collapsed holding 16 London properties; SDB's investment in its assets was £60 million. Breached financial covenants made some debts immediately repayable. — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/market/article/rkwtf2thye)
- Calcalist attributes the losses to rising rates: financing costs grew, values eroded and refinancing became hard. It also notes these investments are usually offered without a prospectus. — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/real-estate/article/hjkyhapsze)
- A Globes-reported lawsuit: "The company promised a return from day one, then everything got complicated." — [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001425760)

**Georgia**
- I found no press report of a specific stalled-project or fraud case involving Israeli investors in Georgia or Batumi. Commercial guides warn about "guaranteed 8–10% yield" on off-plan flats, advise checking title in the Georgian registry (NAPR), and advise against relying on the seller's lawyer. These sources are themselves market players. — [Shalom Georgia](http://shalomgeorgia.co.il/real-estate-scams-in-georgia/); [Elson Group FAQ](https://elsongroup.co.il/faq-georgia-real-estate-investment/)

**What investors now look for (adviser and press guidance)**
- Israel's Sale (Apartments) Law requires a developer bank guarantee and a caveat registration. Most foreign destinations popular with Israelis have no equivalent, so the contract governs and is often one-sided toward the developer. — [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/general/news/article/20027229); [Walla Nadlan](https://nadlan.walla.co.il/item/3204272)
- Guides advise paying into an escrow or trust account managed by an independent trustee lawyer, not directly to the developer or marketer. In Georgia specifically: never pay the developer in cash; use escrow or bank transfers only. — [Moneyplan](https://moneyplan.co.il/%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%A7%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%A0%D7%93%D7%9C%D7%9F-%D7%91%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9C/); [Adv. Yariv, Georgia guide](https://adv-yariv.com/%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%A7%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A0%D7%93%D7%9C%D7%9F-%D7%91%D7%92%D7%90%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%92%D7%99%D7%94/)
- Guides advise asking for a bank guarantee, an Israeli guarantee or a guarantee from the Israeli marketing company, and treat a refusal to give security as a warning sign. — [Shoval Yazamut](https://www.shovalyazamoot.com/blog/%D7%94%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%90%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A0%D7%93%D7%9C-%D7%9F-%D7%91%D7%97%D7%95-%D7%9C)
- They also advise thorough due diligence on the developer's history and completed projects, permits and timelines, and checking the land registry for title, liens and unpermitted building before and after the deal. — [Palmo Group](https://palmo-group.com/buying-a-property-abroad/); [Moneyplan](https://moneyplan.co.il/%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%A7%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%A0%D7%93%D7%9C%D7%9F-%D7%91%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9C/)
- They recommend a local real-estate lawyer in the destination country who is not connected to the developer or marketer, and calculating the full cost (purchase tax, lawyer, translation, checks, bank fees). — [Walla Nadlan](https://nadlan.walla.co.il/item/3204272); [Toprate](https://toprate.co.il/buying-property-abroad-money-transfer/)
- In Dubai there is no per-payment personal bank guarantee as in Israel, but there is "structural security" (per a Dubai-focused marketer). — [Dubai Team](https://dubai-team.co.il/dubai-investment-financing-guide/)

### Inferences
- The collapses share a pattern: pooled or structured vehicles rather than titled units, high leverage, promised regular returns, no prospectus, and money paid to the sponsor. MS Group Global's positioning should stress the opposite: individual title in the buyer's name, escrow or bank guarantees where available, an independent lawyer and no promised yield.
- The MHR settlement and the criminal referral show investors can and do organise collective suits. Reputational risk lasts for years after a single failed project.

### Gaps
- I found no Georgia, Portugal, Romania, Ukraine or Cyprus scandals involving Israeli marketers in reliable press. This does not mean none exist.
- Fetches were blocked, so I could not confirm the exact dates of the HAP and Vision and Beyond reports.

## 5. After-sale services that set the best operators apart

### Takeaway
Leading Israeli overseas-property marketers advertise end-to-end services: legal and banking escort, foreign-buyer permits, rental management, furnishing and staging, ongoing management and tax planning. Independent counsel, overseas mortgage brokerage and investor portals were rarely evident in what I found.

### Cited Findings

## 6. How the best competitors display trust

### Takeaway
Trust displays in this sector are mostly self-published risk pages, FAQs, scam-warning guides and media appearances. I found no evidence of competitors publishing audited track records or full contracts.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Publishing an honest risk section, a scam-warning guide that tells readers how to verify MS Group itself (company number, title-check walkthrough per country, escrow details) and sample contracts would put MS Group Global above most of the sector.
