Privacy policy

This is a translation for your convenience. The Hebrew version is the binding one.

Updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information is collected on the MS Group Global website, what it is used for and what your rights are, in accordance with Israel’s Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, including Amendment 13, and the regulations under it. Publishing this policy is not a legal confirmation of full compliance.

1. Who is responsible for the information

The website is operated under the MS Group Global brand, a subsidiary of M.S GROUP. For questions about personal information, contact us by phone on +972 72-213-3266, by email at office@msgp.co.il, or by post to B.S.R 4 Tower, 7 Metsada St., Bnei Brak, Israel.

The full legal name and registration number of the company that controls the information will be added here.

2. Providing information is your choice

You are under no legal obligation to provide information on the website. You can browse without giving any details, or call us instead. The enquiry form cannot be sent without a name and phone number.

3. What information is collected and why

  • In the enquiry form: full name and phone number (required), budget range and countries of interest (optional).
  • When joining the VIP club: full name, phone number and email, used to send updates and offers about projects, only after you have explicitly agreed to this. You can cancel your membership and unsubscribe at any time.
  • Purpose: handling your enquiry, contacting you, and tailoring our answer to the budget and destinations you chose.
  • You never need to provide an ID number, credit card details, medical information or information about family members on the website.

4. How your enquiry is sent

At present, clicking send opens a ready-made email in your email program, addressed to office@msgp.co.il. The message is only sent if you send it yourself, and it is kept in the company’s mailbox. If the website is later connected to an enquiry management system, this policy will be updated.

5. Marketing messages

Contacting us through the website is not consent to receive advertising. Marketing updates by email, SMS or WhatsApp are sent only to people who explicitly ticked the separate box in the form, in accordance with section 30A of Israel’s Communications (Telecommunications and Broadcasting) Law. Every message lets you unsubscribe, and you can ask to be removed at any time by contacting us.

6. Who receives the information

The information is intended for the MS Group Global and M.S GROUP team in order to handle your enquiry. If details need to be passed to the developer of a specific project abroad to answer your request, we will do so only after telling you who the recipient is and for what purpose, and subject to the law. Information may also be disclosed in response to a valid legal requirement.

7. External services and technical data

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may pass your IP address and browser details to that provider. The website’s hosting service may process technical data such as IP addresses and access times in order to operate and secure the site. A project film embedded from YouTube loads only after you press play, in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com); once it plays, Google’s privacy policy applies to it. The website does not currently use tracking tools or advertising pixels. This policy will be updated before any such tools are used.

8. Retention and security

Information is kept only as long as it is needed to handle your enquiry and stay in touch with you, or as required by law. We take reasonable measures to secure it, including limiting access to the people who need it.

9. Your rights

Under the Protection of Privacy Law you are entitled to review the information about you, to ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete, unclear or out of date, and to ask us to delete it or stop using it for marketing, subject to the law. To make a request, contact us by phone on +972 72-213-3266, by email at office@msgp.co.il, or by post to B.S.R 4 Tower, 7 Metsada St., Bnei Brak, Israel.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date of the latest update appears at the top of this page.
