When an apartment in Israel is out of reach, the opportunity lies overseas

Israeli housing prices have pushed many investors out of the market. Overseas there are excellent alternatives: stable markets, quality projects and an entry point that suits a more modest amount of equity.

For an investment like this to be truly good, you need to know whom to choose. That is why we founded MS Group Global.

Before you invest,
we check

Every developer we work with goes through our checks first. That way you step into a good home, not a gamble.

  1. Financial strength

    Whether the company has the financial backing to complete the project.

  2. Track record

    Completed projects, residents who got their keys, a reputation on the ground.

  3. Area of expertise

    The kind of projects the company is genuinely strong at.

  4. Where it operates

    Where it knows the market, and where it is only trying its luck.

  5. On-time delivery

    Whether the company hands over on schedule, or tends to run late.

  6. Build quality

    The materials, the finish and the standard the apartment will be delivered with.

  7. Price against the competition

    Whether the price is fair compared with the market and similar projects.

Why invest
in each destination

Every market was chosen for a reason. Here are the numbers behind each destination, and what is worth knowing before you go in.

UAE

DUBAI · RAS AL KHAIMAH

A high-yield market, with a new growth engine in Ras Al Khaimah.

  • Dubai: with professionally managed short-term rentals, gross yields in selected projects can reach 9%–15%. For standard long-term rentals the average is about 6.3% (Property Monitor, August 2026).
  • Ras Al Khaimah: apartment values rose about 18% year on year, and about 23% on Al Marjan Island (CBRE, first half of 2026).
  • The Wynn Al Marjan resort is expected to open in 2027 and is drawing demand to the area.

Worth knowingPrice growth in Dubai is slowing, and some neighbourhoods have a large supply. The project’s location is decisive.

Georgia

BATUMI

Among the highest yields of any resort city, in a market Israelis already know.

  • Batumi: with professionally managed short-term rentals, gross yields in selected projects can reach 8%–12%. The city average is about 7.1%–7.4%, among the highest of any resort city (Galt & Taggart).
  • In 2025 Israelis were the fastest-growing group of foreign buyers, at about 13% of transactions (Galt & Taggart).
  • About half of the buyers in the city are foreigners, and the holiday market is busy all summer.

Worth knowingAt the end of 2025 there were about 12,400 unsold apartments in projects, and yields are falling. Choosing the developer and the project is critical.

Cyprus

LIMASSOL · LARNACA

An EU member state using the euro, 40 minutes’ flight from Israel.

  • Housing prices rose about 8.5% year on year, and about 9.8% in Larnaca (Central Bank of Cyprus, Q2 2026).
  • An average gross yield of about 5.5% on apartments (RICS and KPMG index, Q2 2026).
  • A stable European market, with EU law and banking.

Worth knowingLimassol is especially expensive, and the net yield after costs is lower than the gross figure.

Portugal

LISBON

A sought-after European capital with strong capital growth.

  • The median price of homes sold in Portugal rose about 18.4% year on year (INE, Q2 2026).
  • In Lisbon the median price is about €5,200 per m², the highest in the country (INE).
  • An EU member state using the euro, with steady demand from local and foreign buyers.

Worth knowingThe number of transactions fell about 9%, and non-resident buyers pay more on average. It is worth checking the tax position before buying.

Romania

BUCHAREST

A low entry price inside the EU, with sharp price rises.

  • New-build apartment prices in Bucharest rose about 21% year on year, to about €2,600 per m² (Imobiliare.ro index, June 2026).
  • An average gross yield of about 6.9% (Global Property Guide).
  • Roughly half the price per square metre of Lisbon, in an EU member state.

Worth knowingRents are rising more slowly than prices, so the yield on an apartment bought at today’s higher price is lower.

Ukraine

ODESA

A high-risk investment, for investors who understand that risk.

  • In 2025 about five times as many new apartments were delivered in Odesa as in 2024, and demand still exceeds supply (LIGA.net).
  • Prices of new luxury apartments rose about 27% year on year (LUN, June 2026).
  • The main buyers are local residents, not foreign investors.

Worth knowingThe country is at war. This is a very high-risk investment, suitable only for those prepared to take such a risk.

United States

MIAMI

A large, liquid dollar market, in a state with no state income tax.

  • Home sales in Miami-Dade have risen for 11 consecutive months, and sales above $10 million are on course for an annual record (MIAMI REALTORS, 2026).
  • An average gross yield of about 6.8% on apartments in Miami (Global Property Guide).
  • Florida has no state income tax on individuals.

Worth knowingThe median condo price in Miami-Dade dipped slightly over the past year, to about $408,000 (August 2026), and supply is high. Condo association fees and insurance in Florida have risen and need to be factored into the net yield.
