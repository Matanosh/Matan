Accessibility statement

This is a translation for your convenience. The Hebrew version is the binding one.

MS Group Global believes in providing equal service to all clients, and works to make this website accessible in accordance with Israel’s Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 5758-1998, and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013.

The website’s accessibility adjustments aim to meet Israeli Standard SI 5568 at level AA. Automated checks against WCAG 2.1 level AA (the axe tool) and display checks on desktop and mobile have been carried out. These checks do not confirm full compliance with the standard, and a comprehensive manual review with screen readers and a professional accessibility audit have not yet been completed.

Accessibility adjustments on this website

  • Full keyboard navigation, with a clearly visible focus indicator.
  • A “Skip to content” link at the top of every page.
  • A hierarchical heading structure and structural landmarks (header, main content, footer).
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Labels for every form field, and clear error messages next to the field.
  • Colour contrast between text and background in line with the standard.
  • A button to pause the opening animations, and respect for the operating system’s “reduce motion” setting.
  • Layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile, and support for browser text zoom.
  • Project selection through tabs that also work with the arrow keys.
  • The website is available in Hebrew, English and French, with the correct language and reading direction declared on every page.

Known limitations

Some parts of the website are not yet fully accessible. The project films (Wyndham Garden Gonio, Azure by Lapis, PONTUS Gonio, La Quinta by Wyndham, Blue Sky Tower, SORA Beach Residence, One Central, OMYA Residence, VOXA Residence and LINEA by HOLM) do not currently have captions or a full text description. The press coverage is in Hebrew. Project renderings are images, and their full details are provided in the consultation. We are working to improve accessibility, and you can contact us to receive information in an accessible format.
