## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS with no build step. Chosen because the owner is a non-developer working through Claude, the site must host anywhere for free, and every page stays editable as plain files.

## Users

Israeli private investors who want to buy property abroad, in two equal groups:
- First-time investors abroad, who need the whole process explained and want someone to hold their hand from budget to keys.
- Experienced property investors, who are looking for new markets and want to see markets, numbers and a professional operator.

Both read Hebrew; the site is Hebrew, right-to-left.

## Product Purpose

MS Group Global is a subsidiary of MS Group (msgp.co.il). It markets and initiates real-estate projects abroad: Cyprus, Portugal, Romania, Georgia, Dubai and more.

The site exists to do two things:
1. Show professionalism at first sight, so the company reads as a serious, premium operator.
2. Make the visitor understand that MS Group Global is their "home" for investing abroad: one company that handles everything from A to Z, from choosing the right apartment for their budget and goals to handing over the key.

Success: a visitor leaves a consultation request. The site is also the landing destination for project-specific ads that will follow.

## Positioning

Not a listings portal and not a single-project developer: an Israeli company, part of MS Group, that accompanies the investor through the whole journey across several countries, all-inclusive.

## Operating Context

- Ads per project will later drive traffic to the site, so project content must be easy to add as its own block or page.
- The parent site msgp.co.il is the brand reference the owner pointed to. It could not be viewed from the build environment (network blocked); its exact colours and look are still to be confirmed against the real site and logo.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before excitement: every striking moment must also say something true about the service.
2. One home, many countries: the journey is the same everywhere; the destinations change.
3. The process is the product: show the A-to-Z path concretely, step by step.
4. Speak to both the beginner and the experienced investor without talking down to either.
5. Nothing invented: every number and project on the page is real or visibly a slot.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Hebrew RTL throughout; readable for older investors (generous text size and contrast); full experience must work with reduced motion.
