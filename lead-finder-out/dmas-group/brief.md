New-build apartments, marketed directly with no brokerage fees, over 3,000 units, tailored to each buyer's budget.

- Bat Yam: Hana Senesh corner Balfour, by the sea, near Kedem neighborhood in Jaffa
- Ramat Gan: Ruah 149, 50 units
- Givat Shmuel: phase 2, Ben Gurion 10-16, 202 units
- Yehuda HaMaccabi 32-36, 100 units
- Sderot Yad Labanim 10, 14, 31, 33
- Tel Aviv: HaMa'archa 29-31
- Rishon LeZion and more

## Who buys
Private home buyers in Israel (first-time buyers, families upgrading, people moving to the center or to Beit Shemesh) and small property investors. Hebrew speakers.

## The problem, in buyers' words (to search for)
"מחפשים דירה מקבלן", "דירה ראשונה", "בלי עמלת תיווך", "דירה על הנייר", "מחירי הדירות בגבעת שמואל / רמת גן / בת ים", "דירה בבית שמש", "תשלום 20/80", "משכנתא לדירה מקבלן", "משקיע בדירה ראשונה".

## Rules out
Brokers and other developers or marketers, people selling (not buying), renters only, people outside Israel with no plan to buy there. Owner to confirm.

## Legal note
Israel's anti-spam law (Communications Law, section 30A) requires prior consent before sending advertising email or messages to private individuals. Most buyers here are private individuals.
